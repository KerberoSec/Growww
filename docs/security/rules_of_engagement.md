# Rules of Engagement & Safe Harbor Policy

## 1. Principles of Engagement
We welcome responsible security testing from the ethical security research community. To qualify for program rewards and legal safe harbor protection, all activities must adhere to the following mandates.

## 2. Authorized Activities
- Testing must target only assets explicitly defined in `bounty_scope_manifest.yaml`.
- Use test accounts designated with the prefix `bounty_researcher_` or headers containing `X-BugBounty-Hacker: <username>`.
- Immediately halt testing and report findings if customer Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or financial account balances are accessed.

## 3. Explicitly Prohibited Actions
- Denial of Service (DoS/DDoS) attacks against production infrastructure or consensus validators.
- Physical attacks against facilities, data centers, or employees.
- Social engineering, phishing, or vishing of Growww staff or clearing members.
- Front-running real user trades in public or private mempools.
- Destruction, alteration, or public exfiltration of production ledger data.

## 4. Safe Harbor Protection
If activities comply with these Rules of Engagement:
1. We will not pursue civil or criminal legal action under the Indian Information Technology Act (IT Act 2000) or international cybersecurity laws.
2. We will not support law enforcement action regarding authorized research.
3. We will collaborate to understand and validate the report, issuing timely public acknowledgment in our Security Hall of Fame.
