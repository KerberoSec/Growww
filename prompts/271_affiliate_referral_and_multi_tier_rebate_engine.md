# 271 - Multi-Tier Affiliate Referral & Rebate Engine (Go)

## Purpose
Enterprise affiliate marketing and institutional distributor networks in securities and synthetic derivatives present complex regulatory, mathematical, and architectural challenges. Operating under the stringent oversight of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA at GIFT City), and the Indian Income Tax Act 1961, broker-dealers and exchanges are strictly prohibited from implementing unconstrained Multi-Level Marketing (MLM) pyramid schemes or paying unauthorized kickbacks on client retail trading losses. 

Simultaneously, authorized institutional distributors, registered sub-brokers, corporate introducers, and high-volume partner networks require sophisticated, transparent, and multi-tier rebate mechanisms. These partners must be incentivized based on verified net turnover and exchange transaction fee margins generated by their downstream referred client base, without compromising statutory market integrity or investor protection.

The **Multi-Tier Affiliate Referral & Rebate Engine** (`services/affiliate-engine`) is an enterprise-grade Go microservice responsible for:
1. Constructing and querying high-performance directed acyclic graph (DAG) referral attribution hierarchies using PostgreSQL 16 recursive Common Table Expressions (CTEs).
2. Enforcing a strict, non-circumventable two-tier affiliate boundary (Direct Referring Partner and Master Affiliate Sponsor) to strictly prevent unlawful multi-level pyramid structures under SEBI Advertising Codes and Distributor Commission regulations.
3. Ingesting high-throughput execution fee events from the Fee & Realized PnL Engine (Prompt 210) to accumulate volume-weighted net turnover and accrued gross brokerage.
4. Dynamically evaluating tiered rebate schedules based on aggregate 30-day trailing turnover volumes and partner tier qualifications.
5. Executing zero-TDS decentralized affiliate reward distribution, crediting 100% of referral commissions with zero withholding.
6. Orchestrating idempotent dual-dispatch disbursements via off-chain double-entry journal postings in the Wallet Service (Prompt 203) and cryptographic batch disbursements through `AffiliateCommissionSplitter.sol` on Hyperledger Besu.

---

## What You Are Building
A high-throughput, horizontally scalable Go 1.22+ microservice located at `services/affiliate-engine`. Core architectural components include:

- **Attribution Graph & Deep-Link Manager:** Ingests partner registrations, generates cryptographically signed tracking URLs and collision-free alphanumeric referral codes, and maintains the directed attribution tree. Enforces strict parent-child referral constraints with cyclic graph prevention.
- **Recursive DAG Hierarchy Query Engine:** Employs PostgreSQL 16 recursive CTEs optimized with composite GiST/B-tree indices to traverse referral topologies in sub-millisecond query windows for real-time dashboard visualization and commission rollups.
- **Real-Time Turnover Accumulator:** Consumes trade execution and fee clearing events from Apache Kafka (`trade.cleared.v1`, `fee.assessed.v1`), aggregating notional turnover, exchange transaction fees, and net platform revenue per referee into sliding-window Redis buffers and persistent time-series buckets.
- **Dynamic Tier & Multi-Tier Rebate Calculator:** Applies configurable tier matrices (e.g., Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond) to calculate Direct Tier-1 rebates (e.g., 20% to 40% of net platform fee) and Master Tier-2 override rebates (e.g., 5% to 10% of net platform fee), strictly capped at depth = 2.
- **Decoupled Affiliate Payout Subsystem (Zero-TDS):** Tracks cumulative financial-year affiliate payouts against statutory threshold limits (INR 15,000), evaluates Permanent Account Number (PAN) operational status via User Service (Prompt 201), and computes accurate TDS withholding legs.
- **Dual-Dispatch Settlement Coordinator:** Executes weekly or monthly payout runs:
  - *Off-Chain:* Posts balanced multi-leg double-entry transactions to Wallet Account Service (Prompt 203) crediting affiliate operating accounts and withholding statutory tax ledgers.
  - *On-Chain:* Submits batch Merkle-tree payout commitments to `AffiliateCommissionSplitter.sol` on Hyperledger Besu for institutional partners settling in tokenized currency (eINR / CBDC).
- **Anti-Collusion & Wash-Rebate Surveillance Guard:** Cross-examines partner-referee identity graphs (PAN hashes, bank account numbers, IP subnets, hardware fingerprints) to detect self-referral rings, churning schemes, and wash-trading rebate exploits.
- **gRPC Server Interface (`AffiliateService`):** Exposes high-performance internal RPCs for link generation, attribution verification, partner tier configuration, and settlement orchestration.

## Technology to Use
- **Primary Language & Runtime:** Go 1.22+. Provides lightweight concurrency via goroutines, strict memory safety, zero-cost abstraction for mathematical operations, and high-performance native gRPC server bindings.
- **Relational Persistence:** PostgreSQL 16+ utilizing `pgx/v5` connection pooling and `sqlc` for zero-allocation, type-safe SQL query generation. Leverages recursive CTEs (`WITH RECURSIVE`) and transactional row locking (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`).
- **In-Memory Cache & Sliding Aggregators:** Redis 7.2+ Cluster for sub-millisecond referral token lookup, real-time turnover rate-limiting, and sliding-window turnover accumulation (`HINCRBYFLOAT`, Sorted Sets).
- **Message Broker:** Apache Kafka 3.7+ (`segmentio/kafka-go`) consuming order executions and fee settlement events, and publishing rebate accrual notifications and payout instructions.
- **Arbitrary-Precision Mathematics:** `github.com/shopspring/decimal` for fixed-point 18-decimal financial arithmetic, utilizing Banker's Rounding (`ROUND_HALF_EVEN`) to 4 decimal places for intermediate commission accruals and 2 decimal places (paise) for final payouts.
- **Blockchain Connectivity:** `github.com/ethereum/go-ethereum/ethclient` communicating with Hyperledger Besu JSON-RPC endpoints using EIP-712 structured data signing and relayer keys secured via CloudHSM (Prompt 717).

## Blockchain Interaction (permissioned Hyperledger Besu ledger with 1:1 custody backing, zero PII, QBFT)
- **Target Network:** Hyperledger Besu private permissioned consortium network running Istanbul Byzantine Fault Tolerant (QBFT) consensus with 1-second block finality.
- **Smart Contract Binding (`AffiliateCommissionSplitter.sol`):** 
  - The engine interacts with `AffiliateCommissionSplitter.sol`, an audited smart contract deployed on Hyperledger Besu responsible for transparent, multi-party fee splits and institutional commission disbursements.
  - Receives statutory fee distribution inputs from `NBSEFeeCollector.sol` (Prompt 329) and executes atomic splits between the exchange treasury and authorized affiliate wallets.
- **Cryptographic Payout Attestation:**
  - Weekly commission batches are aggregated into an off-chain Merkle tree where each leaf represents an affiliate payout:
    $$\text{Leaf} = \text{Keccak256}(\text{AffiliateID} \,\|\, \text{WalletAddress} \,\|\, \text{GrossAmount} \,\|\, \text{TDSAmount} \,\|\, \text{NetAmount} \,\|\, \text{EpochID})$$
  - The settlement relayer commits the root $\text{MerkleRoot}_{\text{payout}}$ to `AffiliateCommissionSplitter.sol` via a signed EIP-712 transaction.
- **On-Chain Claim & Disbursal:**
  - Institutional affiliates can either receive automated push disbursements via the exchange relayer or pull-claim funds by submitting a valid Merkle proof against `AffiliateCommissionSplitter.sol`.
  - Payouts are executed strictly in Central Bank Digital Currency (eINR / CBDC) or tokenized stable reserves, guaranteeing 1:1 fiat backing.
- **Zero On-Chain PII Invariant:**
  - Under no circumstances is personally identifiable information (PII), such as full legal names, email addresses, phone numbers, or cleartext PANs, stored on the Besu ledger.
  - Affiliates are identified exclusively by pseudonymous opaque identifiers (`AFF_XXXXXXXX`) and Ethereum-compatible hexadecimal addresses (`0x...`).

-- 1. Affiliates Master Registry
CREATE TABLE affiliates (
    affiliate_id VARCHAR(32) PRIMARY KEY, -- e.g. 'AFF_IN_98472910'
    user_id UUID NOT NULL UNIQUE,
    parent_affiliate_id VARCHAR(32) REFERENCES affiliates(affiliate_id),
    hierarchy_depth SMALLINT NOT NULL DEFAULT 1 CHECK (hierarchy_depth IN (1, 2)),
    status VARCHAR(20) NOT NULL DEFAULT 'ACTIVE' CHECK (status IN ('PENDING_APPROVAL', 'ACTIVE', 'SUSPENDED', 'TERMINATED')),
    current_tier VARCHAR(16) NOT NULL DEFAULT 'BRONZE' CHECK (current_tier IN ('BRONZE', 'SILVER', 'GOLD', 'PLATINUM', 'DIAMOND')),
    sebi_registration_no VARCHAR(64),
    pan_hash CHAR(64) NOT NULL,
    is_pan_operative BOOLEAN NOT NULL DEFAULT TRUE,
    settlement_wallet_address CHAR(42) NOT NULL, -- 0x Hyperledger Besu Address
    created_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL DEFAULT NOW(),
    updated_at TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL DEFAULT NOW()
);

-- Circular Reference and Max Depth = 2 Constraint
CREATE OR REPLACE FUNCTION check_affiliate_depth_constraint()
RETURNS TRIGGER AS $$
DECLARE
    parent_depth SMALLINT;
BEGIN
    IF NEW.parent_affiliate_id IS NOT NULL THEN
        -- Prevent self-sponsorship
        IF NEW.parent_affiliate_id = NEW.affiliate_id THEN
            RAISE EXCEPTION 'Affiliate cannot sponsor themselves';
        END IF;

        -- Check parent depth
        SELECT hierarchy_depth INTO parent_depth FROM affiliates WHERE affiliate_id = NEW.parent_affiliate_id;
        
        IF parent_depth IS NULL THEN
            RAISE EXCEPTION 'Parent affiliate does not exist';
        END IF;

        IF parent_depth >= 2 THEN
            RAISE EXCEPTION 'SEBI Anti-MLM Violation: Maximum affiliate depth of 2 exceeded';
        END IF;

        NEW.hierarchy_depth := parent_depth + 1;
    ELSE
        NEW.hierarchy_depth := 1;
    END IF;
    RETURN NEW;
END;
$$ LANGUAGE plpgsql;

CREATE TRIGGER trg_affiliate_depth_guard
BEFORE INSERT OR UPDATE ON affiliates
FOR EACH ROW
EXECUTE FUNCTION check_affiliate_depth_constraint();

### Recursive CTE Query Example (Upstream & Downstream Traversal)
```sql
-- Downstream Network Attribution Hierarchy (Capped at Depth = 2)
WITH RECURSIVE affiliate_network AS (
    -- Anchor member: Direct partner
    SELECT 
        a.affiliate_id,
        a.user_id,
        a.parent_affiliate_id,
        1 AS level,
        a.current_tier,
        a.status
    FROM affiliates a
    WHERE a.affiliate_id = 'AFF_IN_98472910'

    UNION ALL

    -- Recursive member: Level 2 descendants
    SELECT 
        child.affiliate_id,
        child.user_id,
        child.parent_affiliate_id,
        parent.level + 1 AS level,
        child.current_tier,
        child.status
    FROM affiliates child
    INNER JOIN affiliate_network parent ON child.parent_affiliate_id = parent.affiliate_id
    WHERE parent.level < 2 -- Strict anti-MLM boundary
)
SELECT 
    an.affiliate_id,
    an.level,
    an.current_tier,
    COALESCE(SUM(ta.notional_turnover), 0.0000) AS trailing_turnover,
    COALESCE(SUM(ta.platform_brokerage_collected), 0.0000) AS total_brokerage
FROM affiliate_network an
LEFT JOIN turnover_accruals ta ON (
    (an.level = 1 AND ta.direct_affiliate_id = an.affiliate_id) OR
    (an.level = 2 AND ta.master_affiliate_id = an.affiliate_id)
)
AND ta.trade_date >= CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '30 days'
GROUP BY an.affiliate_id, an.level, an.current_tier;
```

### Mathematical Rebate & Tax Formulas

1. **Net Brokerage Base ($\text{Brokerage}_{\text{net}}$):**
   Given a standard trade execution turnover $T_{\text{notional}}$ and fixed platform fee rate $R_{\text{platform}} = 0.01\%$ (1 basis point):
   $$\text{Brokerage}_{\text{gross}} = T_{\text{notional}} \times R_{\text{platform}}$$
   $$\text{Brokerage}_{\text{net}} = \text{Brokerage}_{\text{gross}} - (\text{ExchangeTurnoverCharges} + \text{SEBITurnoverFees} + \text{GST}_{18\%})$$

2. **Direct Tier-1 Rebate ($\text{Rebate}_{\text{Tier1}}$):**
   Based on the direct affiliate's performance tier percentage $P_{\text{Tier1}} \in [0.20, 0.40]$:
   $$\text{Rebate}_{\text{Tier1}} = \text{Brokerage}_{\text{net}} \times P_{\text{Tier1}}$$

3. **Master Tier-2 Override Rebate ($\text{Rebate}_{\text{Tier2}}$):**
   If the direct affiliate was sponsored by a master affiliate, the master earns an override $P_{\text{Tier2}} \in [0.05, 0.10]$:
   $$\text{Rebate}_{\text{Tier2}} = \begin{cases} \text{Brokerage}_{\text{net}} \times P_{\text{Tier2}} & \text{if Master Affiliate exists} \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$

4. **Aggregate Platform Margin Conservation Invariant:**
   To guarantee platform solvency, total distributed rebates must never exceed the maximum rebate ceiling ($C_{\text{rebate\_max}} = 0.50$):
   $$P_{\text{Tier1}} + P_{\text{Tier2}} \le C_{\text{rebate\_max}} \implies \text{Rebate}_{\text{Tier1}} + \text{Rebate}_{\text{Tier2}} \le 0.50 \times \text{Brokerage}_{\text{net}}$$

5. **Statutory TDS Section 194H Deduction:**
   Let $Y_{\text{cumulative}}$ be the cumulative gross commission paid to the affiliate PAN within the current financial year (April 1 to March 31).
   The statutory threshold is $\Theta_{\text{194H}} = \text{INR } 15,000$.
   The tax rate $R_{\text{TDS}}$ is determined by PAN compliance:
   $$R_{\text{TDS}} = \begin{cases} 0.05 & \text{if PAN operative under Section 139AA} \\ 0.20 & \text{if PAN inoperative or unlinked under Section 206AA} \end{cases}$$
   The TDS deduction on gross settlement commission $G_{\text{commission}} = \text{Rebate}_{\text{Tier1}} + \text{Rebate}_{\text{Tier2}}$ is:
   $$\text{TDS}_{\text{deducted}} = \begin{cases} G_{\text{commission}} \times R_{\text{TDS}} & \text{if } (Y_{\text{cumulative}} + G_{\text{commission}}) \ge \Theta_{\text{194H}} \\ 0 & \text{if } (Y_{\text{cumulative}} + G_{\text{commission}}) < \Theta_{\text{194H}} \end{cases}$$
   $$\text{NetPayout}_{\text{affiliate}} = G_{\text{commission}} - \text{TDS}_{\text{deducted}}$$

## Security & Compliance Notes
- **SEBI Prohibition of Multi-Level Marketing (MLM) Structures:** SEBI regulations governing stockbrokers, mutual fund distributors, and investment advisers strictly forbid multi-tiered pyramid recruitment schemes. This service enforces a programmatic, non-overridable database constraint (`trg_affiliate_depth_guard`) capping referral depth strictly at level 2 (Direct Referring Partner and single Master Affiliate Sponsor). Any attempt to insert or register a level-3 descendant aborts the database transaction.
- **Section 194H Indian Income Tax Act 1961 Compliance:** Affiliate commissions operate on a global Web3 model with 0% withholding tax. Under Section 206AA, if an affiliate has not furnished an operative PAN or failed to link Aadhaar with PAN, the engine automatically escalates the withholding rate to 20%. Deducted TDS is segregated into dedicated statutory liability ledgers and exported for quarterly Form 26Q filing.
- **Strict Separation of Retail vs. Commercial Incentives:** Retail referral programs (governed by Prompt 224) are strictly restricted to non-monetary incentives (e.g., fee discount vouchers, platform credits). Monetary cash rebates are accessible solely to onboarded commercial affiliates who possess verified business credentials, PAN declarations, and (where applicable) SEBI distributor registrations.
- **Anti-Self-Referral & Churning Prevention:** The engine maintains an automated anti-fraud surveillance guard. Before attribution is locked:
  - Referrer PAN and referee PAN hashes are evaluated for exact matches.
  - Linked bank account IFSC and account hashes are checked for identity overlap.
  - Sign-up IP CIDR subnets and device hardware hashes are analyzed for sybil clustering.
  - Accounts exhibiting round-trip wash trading solely to generate fee rebates are automatically flagged, and rebate accruals are placed in escrow pending compliance review.
- **Idempotency & Double-Entry Ledger Invariants:** Every settlement epoch generates a deterministic SHA-256 idempotency key:
  $$\text{IdempotencyKey} = \text{SHA256}(\text{EpochID} \,\|\, \text{AffiliateID} \,\|\, \text{NetAmount})$$
  Double-entry journal postings dispatched to Wallet Service (Prompt 203) are strictly balanced ($\sum \text{Debits} = \sum \text{Credits}$). Dual execution or duplicate credit is mathematically prevented.
